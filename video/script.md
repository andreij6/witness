# Witness — pitch video script

**Target:** ≤ 120 s at 1920×1080, 30 fps. Narration is generated with Gemini TTS, one clip per scene; the timing follows the audio.

| # | Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | A storm photo in a social post; skeptical replies pop in | A hailstorm hits your street. You post the photo… and the replies roll in. "AI." "Fake." "Photoshopped." |
| 2 | Problem | "Anything can be faked in seconds." Metadata lines get stripped away | Anything can be faked in seconds now. So real moments get dismissed, fakes get believed, and the metadata that could have helped? Every platform strips it. |
| 3 | Meet Witness | App icon, wordmark, tagline | Meet Witness. Proof, not just pictures. |
| 4 | Seal | Camera screen + three proof points | Shoot in the Witness camera, and your phone's security chip seals the photo the instant you press the shutter: the time, the place, and proof it came from a genuine device. Even offline. |
| 5 | Prepare | Post composer, blur chips highlighted | Before you share, on-device AI finds faces, license plates and house numbers to blur, and keeps your exact location private. None of it leaves your phone. |
| 6 | Survives | Screenshot vs. sealed reference | Then post it anywhere. An invisible watermark and visual fingerprints travel with the image, so even a cropped screenshot traces back to the sealed original, with every change listed. |
| 7 | Honest verdicts | Altered + No record + verdict chips | Someone edits a license plate? Witness highlights exactly what changed. No proof at all? It tells you that too. Not proven isn't the same as fake. |
| 8 | Anyone can check | Desktop proof page + phone | And anyone can check, on any phone or computer, right in the browser. No app. No account. |
| 9 | Cases | Case report + encrypted share | For the moments that really matter, like a fender bender or a move-out, Case mode builds the report for you, and shares it with an end-to-end encrypted link. |
| 10 | Close | Anchored on the Internet Computer → end card | Every proof is anchored on the Internet Computer, a neutral record no one can quietly rewrite. Witness. Capture it. Seal it. Prove it. |

**Voice direction:** warm, confident, conversational narrator in their thirties; unhurried; slight smile; clear emphasis on "Proof, not just pictures" and the closing three beats.
